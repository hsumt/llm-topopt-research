Convergence Behavior
The optimization ran for 178 of 200 allowed iterations and converged via a compliance plateau, achieving an 87.32% reduction in compliance (from 2.3826 to 0.3021). The equilibrium residual at termination is 2.90e-9, well below the 1e-5 relative threshold, confirming tight numerical convergence of the linear solve. However, the density-field change metric at convergence is 0.1966 (L2 change: 0.01506), which is notably above a typical tight topology change tolerance; this indicates the compliance plateau was reached before the density field itself had fully settled, meaning the stopping criterion reflects structural stiffness stabilization rather than complete topological freeze.

Engineering Anomalies
Two anomalies warrant attention. First, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.0 is physically atypical for structural materials (common metals and polymers range from 0.25 to 0.35); this decouples lateral and longitudinal strain coupling entirely and will alter stress distribution compared to a realistic material, potentially biasing member sizing and orientation. Second, the filter radius r_min = 0.05 is very small relative to the element size (element width = Lx/nx = 0.05, element height = Ly/ny = 0.05), meaning the Helmholtz PDE filter length scale is effectively at the mesh resolution limit and provides minimal geometric regularization beyond a single element. Despite this, the grey fraction is 0.1067 (10.67% of elements in the 0.25–0.75 density range), which passes the < 0.35 threshold but is non-negligible for a Heaviside projection run with beta_final = 8.0; a sharper projection would normally push this lower. The density bounds [0.0010, 0.9999] are clean and raise no concern.

Physical Plausibility
For a 3:1 aspect ratio cantilever beam with a downward tip load and a 40% volume fraction target, the achieved compliance of 0.3021 and the volume fraction of 0.3998 are self-consistent and qualitatively expected. The classic cantilever solution should exhibit diagonal strut-and-chord members emanating from the fixed left edge toward the loaded right-center point, and SIMP with penalization of 3.0 under these boundary conditions is well-established to produce such topology. The result is physically plausible, though the nu = 0.0 assumption means the optimized geometry is specific to that material model and should not be interpreted as representative of a real isotropic solid without re-running with a physically realistic Poisson's ratio.

Recommendation
For the next run, increase r_min to at least 1.5–2.0 times the element size (i.e., r_min ≥ 0.075–0.10) to provide meaningful geometric length-scale control and reduce the residual grey fraction; simultaneously raise beta_final to 16.0 or above to sharpen the Heaviside projection further, which should drive the grey fraction below 5% and produce cleaner binary members more suitable for manufacturing interpretation.
