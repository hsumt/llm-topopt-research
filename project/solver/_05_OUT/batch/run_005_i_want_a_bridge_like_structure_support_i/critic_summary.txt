Convergence Behavior
The optimization converged in 81 of the allowed 200 iterations, terminating on the design-change criterion with a final element-wise change of 0.00106 (below the 0.01 tolerance) and an L2 change of 6.66e-05, both indicating tight numerical convergence. Compliance dropped from 0.4566 to 0.03786, a 91.7% reduction, which is a large but not implausible improvement given that the uniform initial density field is far from optimal. The equilibrium residual of 4.18e-10 confirms that the linear solve is well-resolved at termination.

Engineering Anomalies
The maximum density reached is only 0.9435, not 1.0, despite Heaviside projection being active. With a beta_final of 2.0 this is expected behavior — the projection is still relatively soft at this beta value and will not drive densities fully to 0/1. This directly connects to the grey fraction: 24.5% of elements sit in the intermediate range 0.25–0.75, which is below the 35% threshold but is still substantial for a problem where crisp load paths (arches, ties) are physically expected. The minimum density of 0.0025 suggests the void floor (likely rho_min = 0.001 or similar) is being respected, but the soft void is not zero, which is a minor numerical note rather than a failure. No sensitivity-sign violations were detected, confirming SIMP penalization is functioning correctly.

Physical Plausibility
A simply-supported bridge (pinned at both bottom corners, central top load) should develop compression arches running from the load point to the supports and, depending on volume fraction, tensile tie elements near the bottom chord — a topology consistent with a shallow arch-truss hybrid. The 30% volume fraction is aggressive enough to produce a sparse but recognizable structural skeleton. The reported validation status is passed on all checks. The relatively high grey fraction (24.5%) means the current design image likely shows diffuse members rather than crisp bars, so manufacturing interpretability is limited at this beta; the compliance value itself is physically reasonable but corresponds to this intermediate, not fully-binarized, design.

Recommendation
Increase beta_final progressively (e.g., through a continuation schedule reaching beta = 16–32) to sharpen the Heaviside projection, drive maximum densities closer to 1.0, and reduce the grey fraction below 10%. This will improve manufacturing interpretability and yield a compliance value that more faithfully represents a discrete 0/1 design, making the result more actionable for downstream sizing or fabrication.
