Convergence Behavior
The optimization ran for 128 of the allowed 200 iterations and terminated due to a compliance plateau, indicating the objective stagnated before the density-change tolerance of 0.01 was met. Compliance dropped from 0.07411 to 0.01365, an 81.58% reduction, which is a strong improvement. However, the final element-wise change of 0.03351 (L2: 0.00202) exceeds the specified tol_change of 0.01, meaning numerical convergence by the density-change criterion was not achieved; convergence was declared on a secondary plateau criterion instead.

Engineering Anomalies
1. Density change not converged: final_change = 0.03351 is 3.35× the stated tol_change of 0.01. The run stopped on compliance plateau, not on the primary stopping criterion. This is a meaningful distinction — the density field may still be drifting.
2. Grey fraction is elevated: 19.9% of elements fall in the intermediate range (0.25 < ρ < 0.75) against a threshold of 35%. While this passes validation, it is relatively high for Heaviside projection with beta_final = 4.0, which is a low final beta value and likely explains the residual greyness.
3. Maximum density reaches only 0.9874, not 1.0. Combined with the grey fraction and low beta, the Heaviside sharpening has not driven elements to fully solid regions, suggesting projection is under-aggressive.
4. Minimum density is 0.0086 (not true void). This is consistent with the low beta but should be noted if the topology is to be interpreted as a crisp 0/1 design.
5. Equilibrium residual of 1.81 × 10⁻⁹ is well within tolerance, so the FE solve itself is sound.

Physical Plausibility
For a 2D cantilever beam (aspect ratio 1.6:1, fixed left edge, downward point load at right center, 40% volume fraction), the expected topology is the well-known arch-and-diagonal truss pattern with a strong bottom chord in tension, top chord in compression, and diagonal members. An 81.58% compliance reduction at 40% material usage is physically consistent with this problem class. The validation suite passes all checks, including correct sensitivity sign everywhere (dc/dρ ≤ 0), confirming the physics and adjoint sensitivities are self-consistent. No basis exists in this packet to question the physical correctness of the load/boundary condition setup.

Recommendation
Increase beta_final substantially — from 4.0 to at least 16 or 32 — and introduce a beta-continuation schedule (e.g., doubling beta every 20–30 iterations starting from beta = 1) to drive the Heaviside projection toward a sharper 0/1 field. This should reduce the grey fraction below 10%, push maximum densities toward 1.0, close true voids to near 0.0, and is likely to allow the density-change criterion (tol_change = 0.01) to be met rather than relying on a compliance plateau stop, yielding a more manufacturable and numerically converged result.
