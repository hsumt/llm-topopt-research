3. Density bounds reach only [0.001, 0.997], not the sharp 0/1 limits expected from a beta = 16 Heaviside projection; this is consistent with the elevated grey fraction and incomplete binarization.
4. The filter radius r_min = 0.1 on a domain of Lx = 3.0 corresponds to approximately 2 elements in the y-direction but only ~2 elements in x out of 60, making the filter anisotropically weak along the long axis.

Physical Plausibility

An 83.5% compliance reduction with volume constrained to 40% is plausible for this load case. However, the 30.5% grey fraction means a significant portion of the design domain is not clearly solid or void; this would be unmanufacturable as stated and makes precise physical interpretation of the topology premature. The validation status is passed per the provided checks, but numerical convergence has not been achieved.
