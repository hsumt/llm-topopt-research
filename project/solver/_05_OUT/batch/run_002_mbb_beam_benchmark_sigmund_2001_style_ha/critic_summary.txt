Convergence Behavior
The optimizer ran for 189 of a maximum 400 iterations and terminated on a compliance plateau, indicating the objective stalled before the formal density-change tolerance of 0.01 was satisfied. The final density change was 0.0285 (above the 0.01 threshold) while the L2 change was 0.00141, suggesting macro-scale density redistribution had largely ceased but local element-level oscillations had not fully died out. Compliance dropped from 0.3293 to 0.0406, an 87.66% reduction, which is consistent with a well-progressing SIMP run on this problem class.

Engineering Anomalies
The stopping criterion deserves scrutiny: convergence was declared on a "compliance plateau" rather than on the density-change tolerance (final_change = 0.0285 > tol_change = 0.01), meaning the run was stopped by a secondary heuristic, not the primary criterion. The maximum density cap of 0.9988 (instead of 1.0) and minimum of 0.0020 (instead of the void floor) are minor but indicate the Heaviside projection at beta = 8.0 has not fully driven densities to crisp 0/1 values everywhere; this is consistent with the reported grey fraction of 8.17%, which, while within the validation threshold of 35%, is non-negligible and could affect manufactured stiffness. No anomalies in sensitivity sign or equilibrium residual (2.17e-9, well below 1e-5) were detected.

Physical Plausibility
The MBB beam setup — point load at top-left, horizontal roller on the left edge, vertical pin at bottom-right — is the standard half-symmetry MBB configuration. An 87.66% compliance reduction at 40% volume fraction with penalization p = 3.0 is physically reasonable and consistent with published benchmarks for this problem at similar mesh resolutions (120 × 40). The grey fraction of 8.17% is acceptable but implies some intermediate-density members that may not translate cleanly to manufacturable geometry; the physical load paths are likely well-defined in the dense regions but blurred at member boundaries. No evidence of checkerboarding pathology is apparent given the Helmholtz PDE filter with r_min = 0.06 (approximately 2.4 elements at this mesh scale).

Recommendation
For the next run, increase beta_final beyond 8.0 (e.g., to 16 or 32) within a continuation scheme that ramps beta more aggressively in the later iterations, specifically to push the 8.17% grey fraction lower and drive densities closer to true 0/1; simultaneously, re-examine the compliance-plateau stopping heuristic and either tighten its trigger or replace it with the primary density-change criterion at tol_change = 0.01 to ensure termination reflects genuine design convergence rather than objective stagnation.
